Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026 · Effective immediately

At BackendEngineer ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your details when you visit our website and subscribe to our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when creating an account, subscribing to our membership, or contacting our team:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password hash, and professional preferences.
  • Billing & Payment Information: Payment transactions are processed securely through certified payment gateways (Razorpay, PayPal). We do not store your full credit card numbers, CVV, or banking passwords on our servers.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, searches performed, device type, browser information, and referral URLs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly to provide and enhance our services:

  • To provide access to premium backend engineering jobs, salary insights, and study guides.
  • To process transactions and send payment receipts.
  • To notify you about important service updates, job alerts, or security notices.
  • To maintain system performance and prevent fraudulent activity.

3. Third-Party Payment Processing

We use Razorpay and PayPal as authorized payment gateways. When making a payment, your data is transmitted directly via encrypted SSL connections (TLS 1.3 / 256-bit encryption) adhering to PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance standards. Please refer to Razorpay's and PayPal's respective privacy policies for details regarding their security protocols.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies to remember your login session and theme preference (Dark/Light mode). You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of our website may not function as intended.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard physical, electronic, and managerial safeguards to prevent unauthorized access. We retain your information only as long as your account remains active or as required by statutory regulations.
