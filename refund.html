Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026 · Effective immediately

🎯 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to be completely satisfied with our platform. If you are not satisfied with your membership, you can request a full refund within 7 days of purchase — no questions asked.

1. Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your subscription at any time:

  • You may cancel directly through your account dashboard or by emailing our support team at support@backendengineer.com.
  • Upon cancellation, your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period, and no further recurring charges will occur.
  • There are zero cancellation fees.

2. Refund Eligibility

We provide full refunds under the following guidelines:

  • Initial Subscriptions: Requests made within 7 days of your initial purchase date are eligible for a 100% full refund.
  • Double Charges or Technical Errors: If you experience duplicate billing due to a technical or payment gateway glitch, we will immediately refund the duplicate charge.
  • Employer Job Posts: Once a featured job post has been actively published and distributed on the platform for more than 48 hours, it is not eligible for a refund.

3. Refund Processing & Timeline

Once your refund request is received and verified by our support team:

  • We initiate the refund immediately via our payment gateway (Razorpay or PayPal).
  • The refunded amount will be credited back to your original payment method (credit card, debit card, UPI ID, or net banking account).
  • Depending on your banking institution, funds typically reflect in your account within 5 to 7 business days.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please send an email to support@backendengineer.com with the subject line "Refund Request", including:

  • Your registered account email address
  • Payment Transaction ID or Razorpay Payment ID
  • Brief reason for cancellation (optional, helps us improve!)
